\section{Committing the witness}\label{sec:rs}

\subsection{Multilinear stacking}\label{sec:stacking}

Suppose we want to commit to $T$ multilinears $P_1,\dots,P_T$ over boolean hypercubes of various sizes. We could commit to each independently, at the cost of a large cumulative opening proof. Instead they are concatenated into a large multilinear polynomial $q$, which we commit to with an inner-product commitment scheme (Definition~\ref{def:ipcs}), with a protocol to reduce opening claims on $P_i$ to a claim on $q$. Order them by size, $\kappa_1\ge\dots\ge\kappa_T$ variables, and concatenate their values end to end,
\[
  q \;=\; P_1 \,\Vert\, P_2 \,\Vert\,\dots\,\Vert\, P_T \,\Vert\, 0,
\]
zero-padded to length $2^{M}$, with $\nstack:=\textstyle\sum_i 2^{\kappa_i}$ the number of placed field elements and $M=\lceil\log_2\nstack\rceil$. Placed largest first, $P_i$ starts at an offset that is a multiple of $2^{\kappa_i}$; writing $\mathsf{sel}_i\in\cube{M-\kappa_i}$ for the high bits of that offset,
\[
  \widetilde P_i(z)\;=\;\widetilde q(z,\mathsf{sel}_i)\qquad(z\in\cube{\kappa_i}).
\]
An evaluation claim $\widetilde P_i(\zeta)=c$ is therefore a claim on the stack,
\[
  \widetilde q(\zeta,\mathsf{sel}_i)\;=\;\sum_{w\in\cube{M}}\eq\bigl((\zeta,\mathsf{sel}_i),w\bigr)\,\widetilde q(w)\;=\;c,
\]
a weighted sum over $\widetilde q$ with weight $W(w)=\eq((\zeta,\mathsf{sel}_i),w)$.

\paragraph{Batching.} By the identity above, the claims the protocol raises on the $P_i$ transfer to a list of claims $\langle W_j,\widetilde q\rangle=c_j$ on the stack, with $\E$-valued weights and targets. The verifier then samples $\lambda\in\E$, and it remains to prove
\[
  \Bigl\langle \sum_j\lambda^{j-1}W_j,\;\widetilde q\Bigr\rangle\qeq\sum_j\lambda^{j-1}c_j
\]
via the PCS (Annex~\ref{annex:pcs}).

\subsection{Committing to booleans via Ring-Switching}\label{sec:ringswitch}

The Boolean witnesses of both RV64IM table circuits and Flock's BLAKE2s argument (\S\ref{sec:tab-blake2s}) use ring switching~\cite{DP24}. For an RV64IM table of height $2^\tau$ with $2^{\kpacked}$ Boolean wires per row, $\zpacked$ has $\kpacked+\tau$ coordinates. Pack its low $\kskip=6$ wire coordinates into one $\K$ element, giving $\qpacked$ in $\kpacked+\tau-6$ coordinates. Consecutive wire bits occupy consecutive polynomial-basis bits, the remaining wire coordinates precede the row coordinates, and each table has its own packed region. The optional BLAKE2s witness similarly occupies the $\qflock$ region described in Annex~\ref{annex:rs}.

The native stack contains the public ROM finalize counts and, for each present table, its physical $\K$ columns and packed Boolean witness. Only columns referenced by bus flushes, base-field relations, or BLAKE slot links are physical columns; internal Boolean wires do not each occupy a $\K$ column. Their connection to the physical words is proved by the alias-projection reduction (\S\ref{sec:packed-projection}). If BLAKE2s is present, its separate Flock witness is committed too, and the eighteen physical input/output limbs are linked to Flock slots by opening claims. Absent tables contribute no columns or packed region.

Each present table produces two $64$-slice families on the same packed region: one from R1CS validity and one from physical-word projection. BLAKE2s adds one more family on $\qflock$ when present. After all families are bound, one shared ring map reduces each family to one weighted claim. The final $\lambda$ batching combines all these ring claims and the ordinary physical-column claims into one WHIR opening. The public layout fixes stable tie-breaking, placement, and PCS lane padding (\S\ref{sec:e2e-unrolled}); the generic stacking identity above is unchanged.

The dedicated field-native recursion circuit is a separate relation. Its field-operation tables commit three $\K$ coefficients per private $\E$ port, with a separate fixed commitment authenticating circuit metadata and wiring. Its BLAKE2s table still uses a packed Flock witness and ring switching; it does not add an RV64IM packed Boolean circuit to every field operation. Both the private and fixed claims are opened with WHIR (\S\ref{sec:e2e-unrolled}).
